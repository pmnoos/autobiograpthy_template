Frequently Asked Questions

I'm not very good with computers — is this still for me? +

Yes. That's exactly who this is for. We handle everything technical — you don't need to touch a single setting, password, or account. All you need to do is share your memories with us; we'll do the rest.

What if I want to stop after a few chapters? +

That's completely fine. You only pay for the chapters you've written. There's no minimum, no contract, and no pressure to finish a certain number. Your site is yours to grow whenever — and however much — you'd like.

How long does it take? +

That's really up to you. Some people write a chapter every week; others take a few months between chapters while memories come back to them. We never rush you — take all the time you need.

What do I need to send you for each chapter? +

Just a few sentences about a memory, event, or person — as much or as little detail as you're comfortable with. We'll ask a few gentle follow-up questions if we need more, and turn what you give us into a written chapter.

Who owns the finished site? +

You do. It's your story, hosted under your own site, from day one.

Can I add photos? +

Yes — a photo gallery is available as an optional extra, priced separately once you know what you'd like included.

What if I get stuck or have a question partway through? +

Just get in touch. We're happy to help at any stage — that's part of the service, not something you'll be charged extra for.
